About

Who is Sandun Dias?

Psychological counsellor, transformational mindset & performance coach and corporate trainer.

Sandun Dias is the Founder & CEO of Dream Squad Training Institute. Using NLP, Hypnotherapy and Time Line Therapy®, he helps individuals, professionals and organisations in Sri Lanka rethink, reframe and move towards lasting change.

What sets him apart is a simple belief: real growth is shared. He grows by helping others grow, and brings people along on a journey towards their own success.

My story

Growing, together

I came to realise that changing myself alone was never enough. For me, real growth means growing while helping others grow, because when people change, they carry that change into their families, workplaces and the world around them.

I believe I have unique strengths, and they are meant to be shared. That is why I coach, counsel and train: to bring people along on this journey, help them unlock their own potential, and be a part of their success every step of the way.
